# Example: team message

Fictional example. The system, concern and suggested next step are invented to
illustrate a short group message. No test result or team agreement is implied.

---

Hi team,

I like the idea of a shared deployment checklist. It should make handovers easier
and reduce the number of steps each team has to recreate.

One point I would clarify before we adopt it is the distinction between checks
that always apply and those that depend on the service. A single long checklist
could lead people to mark steps as complete even when they are not relevant.

Could we keep a short common checklist and let each service add its own checks,
with an owner and a clear expected result? We could try it on one routine change
and one rollback exercise before making it the default.

That would keep the process consistent without asking every service to follow
steps that do not fit its deployment model.
